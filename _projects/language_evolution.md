This term paper titled **Evolutionary Drivers and Neural Underpinnings of Bilingualism** was part of the **Language Evolution** seminar offered by Saarland University for the Winter Semester of 2023/24 taught by [Dr. Pablo Contreras Kallens](https://www.contreraskallens.com/).

In this paper, I wrote about the the evolutionary pressures on humans to acquire a new language other than their own mother tongue, how the brain helps humans achieve this and in turn, how the phenomenon of bilingualism affects the evolution of languages itself. I touch upon topics like evolution of bilingualism and evolutionary pressures for bilingualism, age of acquistion, brain and nueral plasticity in my paper. 

This is indeed an very ambitious paper to fit everything within 8 pages but it was super fun to read some of the interesting research papers while working on this. I highly recommend reading [Wray and Grace, 2007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lingua.2005.05.005). The title of their paper itself is compelling: "The consequences of talking to strangers: Evolutionary corollaries of socio-cultural influences on linguistic form".
