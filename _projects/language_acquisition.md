This term paper titled **Input Word Frequency Effects on the Noun and Verb Learning in First Language Acquisition** was part of the **Acquisition of Spoken Language** seminar offered by Saarland University for the Summer Semester of 2024 taught by [Prof. Dr. Bernd Möbius](https://www.coli.uni-saarland.de/~moebius/mywww/index.html).

This paper explored the effects of input frequency of words on children during the lexical acquisition of nouns and verbs. There is a wide range of input frequency effects on various domains of language including phonology and phonotactics, morphology, morphosyntax, syntax and word order. The usage-based linguistics emphasizes a lot on the effects of environment, input and usage on the language learning and usage. This paper is about observing the acquisition of nouns and verbs in the lens of usage-based theories. 

However, reading the studies that uses children as subjects to understand acquisition makes me wonder if we are uncovering the details (not doubting the results but I feel our accounts maybe incomplete). But we deal with what is best available and I'd say that's our stance here too. It would super interesting to see the effects of frequency in bilingual environment (I haven't looked at the papers, but I am certain there are works related to this angle).
