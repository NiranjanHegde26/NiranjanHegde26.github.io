This term paper titled **Exploring Lifelong Language Learning: From Human Brain to Artificial Intelligent Models** was part of the **NLP For Psycholinguistic Research** seminar offered by Saarland University for the Winter Semester of 2023/24 taught by [Dr. Pablo Contreras Kallens](https://www.contreraskallens.com/).

I wrote about the theories and studies that try to understand the human competence to remember (or forget) various aspects of language learning for a longer period, the factors that affect the memory and forgetting. Later, based on the observations in human, I covered ideas from characteristics of artifical neural networks in terms of their meory retention and the efforts to avoid catastrophic forgetting (a phenomenon where the previously learnt knowledge is precipitously forgotten or erased with the learning of new knowledge).

This paper was my first attempt at writing a research-paper-style report and as expected, I was sloppy. In spite of this, I felt that ideas presented with regards to language learning and memory are fascinating. I spend a lot of time talking about sleep and memory, where the papers are focused on the advantages of sleep in the learning process and memory. This is certainly a domain that is worth exploring further, and I am certainly willing to pick such works in future.
